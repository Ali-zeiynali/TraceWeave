# Code of Conduct

TraceWeave contributors should keep project spaces professional, constructive and focused on the work.

We expect participants to:

- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and unwanted personal targeting;
- protect credentials and private information;
- give reproducible technical reports where possible;
- respect maintainers' decisions about security, legal and operational scope.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, irrelevant or privacy-invasive content and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe.
