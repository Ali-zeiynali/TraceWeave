## Skill: public person research

When the subject is a person, solve identity resolution before merging records.

- Establish canonical name, native-script spelling, transliterations, public professional affiliations and date ranges.
- Search aliases and usernames across public web, code, publications, conference pages and public social profiles.
- Collect public posts, replies/comments, talks, repositories, papers, interviews and credited media that bear on the requested angle.
- Check organization pages, coauthor lists, event schedules and archived pages for relationships and former affiliations.
- Inventory relevant public images and documents; OCR visible text, inspect metadata and compare perceptual duplicates before optional vision.
- Compare dates, employers, locations at coarse public/professional level and content style to reject namesakes.
- Preserve conflicting candidates as separate entities and attach the evidence that supports each possible match.
- Use profile-photo similarity only as a discovery hint, never as proof of identity.

Do not contact the subject or third parties. If reliable evidence shows the subject is a minor, exclude the subject from collection.
