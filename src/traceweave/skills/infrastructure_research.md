## Skill: passive infrastructure research

For organization/domain work:
- Extract domains from primary records and discovered pages rather than guessing ownership.
- Query certificate transparency, RDAP, DNS-over-HTTPS, passive subdomain sources and RIPE/PeeringDB context.
- Enumerate historical and current subdomains through passive indexes and archives.
- Link domains, IPs, ASNs, certificates, repositories, documentation and legal entities with dated evidence.
- Inspect public DNS record changes, redirects, sitemaps, robots files and archived hosts for product/project clues.
- Track confidence in ownership separately from technical co-hosting or shared-provider relationships.
- Store the discovery path and raw registry/API response for every infrastructure assertion.

Keep this workflow passive. A DNS lookup or existing-scan lookup is evidence collection; port scanning, brute force and takeover testing are not part of autonomous research.
