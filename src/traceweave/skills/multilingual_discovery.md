## Skill: multilingual discovery

Always build a language/alias matrix when the subject, country or evidence suggests a non-English context.
- Keep at least one English query as a bridge to global indexes.
- Add the user's language, the subject's native language/script and common transliterations.
- Use native legal suffixes, job titles, product terminology, social vocabulary and date formats.
- Search exact native names and aliases separately; do not rely on machine transliteration alone.
- Feed newly discovered spellings, handles, acronyms and translated product terms into later rounds.
- Record which language produced each source so the coverage audit can expose blind spots.

Native-language results are not automatically more authoritative, and English results are not automatically broader.
Cross-check identity and claims across scripts with dates, affiliations, domains and other evidence before merging.
