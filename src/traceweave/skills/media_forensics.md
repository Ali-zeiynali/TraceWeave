## Skill: media and visual evidence

For every relevant image, video frame or scanned document, use the cheapest deterministic layer first:

1. Preserve the original URL, source, timestamp, content hash and local artifact path.
2. Extract file/container metadata and dimensions; never treat editable EXIF as independently verified.
3. OCR in likely languages and store exact text with a region/page locator and confidence.
4. Compute exact and perceptual hashes to group duplicates, crops and reposts.
5. Use reverse-image result imports or indexed filename/caption searches to find earlier versions and context.
6. Send only high-value unresolved regions to vision; ask for visible observations, not identity or unsupported conclusions.

Never identify a person solely from a face. Omit regions containing a known or apparent minor.
Treat codenames, screens, badges, logos, documents and equipment as leads; corroborate rare/high-impact visual observations with another source.
